Yu Jin, Juhyeok Yoon, James Andrew Self, and Kyungho Lee. From Inspiration to Conceptualization – Investigating the Use of Generative AI in Fashion Design for Early-Stage Concept Visualization. Design Research Society Conference (DRS’24). Boston, USA. https://doi.org/10.21606/drs.2024.680

Abstract

The fashion industry is evolving rapidly, and designers must adapt by continually finding innovative ways to stay creative. One promising tool is generative AI, technology that can create images from text descriptions. Fashion designers are beginning to utilize AI programs like Midjourney and Stable Diffusion to aid in early idea development. These tools enable designers to quickly generate visual sketches based on their ideas, which can expedite the process of creating new clothing concepts. However, we don’t yet fully understand how designers use these AI tools in practice—what works, what doesn’t, and how these tools affect creativity. This study examines the interactions between professional designers and generative AI, particularly during the initial “inspiration” phase of the design process. It examines how designers create prompts for the AI, the types of images that are most effective, and how the AI’s output impacts their decisions. The goal is to learn how AI can become a helpful partner in creative work, supporting rather than replacing the designer’s imagination.
